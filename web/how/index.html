Architecture · trust · limits

How It Works

Three contracts and one enclave program. Here is what each does, who you have to trust, and what this build does not do yet.

One join, end to end

The page prepares a challenge

Subject bg2 sha256(guild:wallet:salt) expiry. The salt stays in your browser, so the subject reveals nothing on its own.

You send it from your mailbox

To any other address. Your mail host signs it with DKIM. Mail to yourself is not signed by Gmail, so it would not work.

The page seals it

Unsigned headers are dropped, then the email and salt are encrypted (X25519, HKDF-SHA256, ChaCha20-Poly1305) to the guild's inbox key.

sodalis.testnet calls OutLayer

join forwards the sealed letter with the DKIM key the DAO pinned for that domain, the joining wallet, and the hash of the verifier build it trusts. The transaction yields until the enclave answers.

The verifier runs in Intel TDX

It refuses unless the run came from the guild and holds exactly the guild's secret. It opens the letter and checks the RSA-2048+ signature, that From, To and Subject are signed, domain alignment, a single mailbox, no role address, the subject's commitment to this wallet and guild, and expiry. It returns the domain and an HMAC of the mailbox under the guild master.

The guild decides, the DAO seats

In the callback the guild rejects a reused fingerprint, records the member, then proposes and approves the seat with its gatekeeper role. It reads the DAO's status back before counting the seat as held.

Who you trust

PartyWhat they could doWhat stops them
Your mail hostSign an email for any address on its domain.Nothing. DKIM means trusting the domain's mail host, as email does today.
OutLayer operatorWithhold or delay a run.It cannot forge one with an approved worker image: Intel's quote binds the code, the input and the output, and the proof page checks it.
OutLayer's register ownerApprove a malicious worker image, which could then forge results.The approved list is public on worker.outlayer.testnet; the proof page shows which approved image ran. This is the deepest trust assumption.
The DAO councilAdd or remove an approved domain, change the DAO policy.Every change is a public proposal. Once the issuer list is sealed, no key can be added at all.
The builderRedeploy the guild while its account still has a key.Deleting that key is the last step before submission. The guild already owns its secret, so after that nobody can change the code or the master.
Anyone reading the chainSee the domain, the wallet and a fingerprint.The fingerprint is keyed by a master that never leaves the enclave, so a guessed address cannot be tested against it.
