NEAR testnet · about five minutes

Join

One email from an approved domain earns a seat. Your browser makes a fresh key, seals the email so only the enclave can read it, and the guild seats you in the DAO. No wallet to install.

Before you start · about ten minutes

  • A computer or tablet. Saving an email as a file is hard on a phone.
  • A Gmail or Proton address to send from.
  • A second inbox you can open to receive it: another Gmail account works.

Five steps: make a key, fund it, email yourself a code, upload that email, then ask the fund. Each step turns green when it is done.

  1. I

    Your member key

    A brand-new NEAR account that exists only in this browser. It is not linked to any wallet you already use.

    —

  2. II

    Gas from the guild

    The guild's drip sends 0.3 test NEAR to each new key, so you never move funds from a wallet you already own. Joining and one request spent about 0.01 in our run; the rest stays in your key.

    Public: anyone can see that this key was funded by the guild's drip, and when.

  3. III

    Send the challenge

    Copy the line below. From your Gmail or Proton, write a new email to your other inbox and paste the line as the subject. The message itself can say anything. Gmail does not sign mail sent to the same address, so it has to be a second inbox.

    —

    Approved domains: reading…. Then open the copy that arrived and save it as a file. In Gmail: the ⋮ menu on the message → Download message. Elsewhere, look for Show original or Download message.

  4. IV

    Seal it and join

    Your browser keeps only the signed headers and encrypts the email to the guild's inbox key before it leaves this page. Only the enclave can open it.

    Public: your key, the domain, a keyed fingerprint of the address, the sealed bytes and the time. Not public: the address itself.

    • V

      Ask the treasury

      The DAO funds a round with a total budget and the same allowance for every seat. You ask through the guild, which refuses anything over your allowance or the round's budget and files the rest for staff to decide. You cannot approve your own request.

      Public: the amount, the category and every vote. There is no free-text field, so nothing else about you can be published.
