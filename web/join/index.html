NEAR testnet · about five minutes

Join

One email from an approved domain earns a seat. Your browser makes a fresh key, seals the email so only the enclave can read it, and the guild seats you in the DAO. No wallet to install.

  1. I

    Your member key

    A brand-new NEAR account that exists only in this browser. It is not linked to any wallet you already use.

    —

  2. II

    Gas from the guild

    The guild's drip sends 0.3 test NEAR to every new key, so every member's funding looks the same. Most of it is a gas reserve and comes back.

  3. III

    Send the challenge

    From your mailbox, send an email with exactly this subject to any other address you can read. Gmail does not sign mail you send to yourself, so use a second inbox.

    —

    Approved domains: reading…. Then open the copy that arrived and save it as a file. In Gmail: the ⋮ menu on the message → Download message. Elsewhere, look for Show original or Download message.

  4. IV

    Seal it and join

    Your browser keeps only the signed headers, encrypts the email to the guild's inbox key and sends it. The enclave checks it; the chain only ever sees your domain and a fingerprint.

    • V

      Ask the treasury

      Members can propose a payout. Public members cannot approve their own; staff decide.
